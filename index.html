Aethrion Shorts Uploader

Aethrion Shorts Uploader is a private, single-user tool that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It publishes short educational science and space videos created for the AETHRION INFINITY YouTube channel.

What the app does

  • Once a day, it prepares one short video (script, narration, captions and public-domain NASA imagery) on the owner's computer.
  • It uploads that video to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API, and schedules it to go public at a set time.
  • It only acts on the single YouTube channel owned by the person who runs it. It has no other users and no public sign-up.
