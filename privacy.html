Privacy Policy

Aethrion Shorts Uploader · Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how Aethrion Shorts Uploader (“the app”), operated by Suhas M for the AETHRION INFINITY YouTube channel, handles information. The app is a private tool used only by its owner on their own computer.

1. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to, and schedule videos on, the owner's YouTube channel. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information the app accesses

  • Google account authorization: when the owner signs in once with Google, the app receives an OAuth access token and refresh token for the YouTube scopes it requests (uploading and managing videos on the owner's channel).
  • Video upload details: the video file, title, description, tags and scheduled publish time that the app itself creates.
  • Upload results: the video ID that YouTube returns, used to avoid uploading the same video twice.

The app does not access viewers' data, comments, subscribers, analytics, messages, contacts or any other user's information.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to upload and schedule the owner's own videos on the owner's own channel. It is not used for advertising, profiling, selling or any other purpose.

4. Storage

Tokens and upload records are stored only on the owner's own computer, in local files that are not shared or synced to any server operated by the app. No information is stored on any external server operated by the app.

5. Sharing

Information is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube, as needed to perform the upload.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Deleting the app's folder from the computer permanently deletes all stored tokens and records. To request deletion or ask a question, email the contact below; requests are handled within 7 days.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect information from children.

9. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
